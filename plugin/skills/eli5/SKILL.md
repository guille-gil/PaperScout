# ELI5

If a specific paper is named, ground the explanation in it (Paper Scout `paper` with `detail=tldr`, then `read` for the section map and one or two sections). If only a topic is given, anchor on the clearest or most important of 1 to 3 representative papers.

Answer inline (no file unless asked) with these headings: One-Sentence Summary, Big Idea, How It Works, Why It Matters, What To Be Sceptical Of, If You Remember 3 Things.

Short sentences, concrete words, jargon defined at once or removed, one good analogy rather than several weak ones. Separate what the paper shows from interpretation. End with the source and the `Next:` line.
